## Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

App Name: Equil

This Privacy Policy describes how Equil ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and handles your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
- We collect only the minimum amount of data necessary to provide you with a functional and personalized fitness tracking experience:

- Google Account Information: When you sign in using your Google account, we collect your basic profile information (such as your name and email address) to authenticate your identity and create your Equil account.

- Fitness and Health Data: To calculate calorie burn and track your physical activity, Equil requests access to step count data. On Android, this is retrieved via Health Connect; on Apple devices, this is retrieved via Apple HealthKit. This data is processed to provide core app features.

- Images and Scans: Users can choose to capture or upload images manually for logging, or utilize our AI scanner feature. Images submitted to the AI scanner are transmitted securely to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your scan results and estimate nutritional or fitness metrics.

- App Logs and Preferences: We save local app logs and configuration data to make logging in smoother, maintain your session across app restarts, and help restore your preferences if you switch to a new device.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- We use the collected information exclusively for the following purposes:

- To authenticate your account and securely manage your login sessions via Supabase.

- To process manual logs and transmit images to the Gemini AI scanner to deliver accurate food, activity, or fitness insights.

- To sync your fitness logs and preferences so they remain accessible if you sign in on a new device.

- To process health metrics (step counts) to estimate calorie burn and display your fitness progress.

## 3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services
- Your account authentication and sync data are securely managed using Supabase as our backend infrastructure. Images processed through the AI scanner are handled by Google's Gemini API in accordance with standard enterprise data privacy standards. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

## 4. Account Deletion and Data Control
- You maintain complete control over your personal data:

- Account Deletion: You can delete your account at any time directly through the app settings.

- Data Removal: Deleting your account permanently removes your account credentials and associated synced data from our Supabase servers. Local data stored on your device can be completely cleared by uninstalling the application.

## 5. Contact Us
- If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, you can reach out via the support contact available in the app settings.
